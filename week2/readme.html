Sketch Name

I know it’s probably a little bit overdecorated, but it’s hard to give up an idea after it has already come to mind. And I promise it just uses three kinds of simple shapes (ellipse, rectangle, triangle), and there is nothing outside what we learn in class. I’m trying to use those simple 2D shapes to create a sense of depth and establish a visual centerpiece. I divide the canvas into four parts, and each one will have a shape moving randomly, and use fill/nofill triangles based on those random shapes' points’ positions to make it look more three-dimensional. Then the center of the pattern will be full of straight lines, so I need to add some circles on the background so that the whole style will look more harmonious, which can also make the front part stand out.
